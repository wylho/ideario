Organize your life in notes.

A free, local-first notes app with reminders, a calendar and a kanban board in one place: write once and see it however you need. It opens instantly, works offline and, if you want, syncs through your own Google Drive.

One note, six views

Write once. See it however you need.

Everything starts as a note. The rest are views of it, and nothing is copied: change it in one place and it changes everywhere. Categories and tags filter any view.

Notes

Colorful cards, pinned to the top, in whatever order you drag them.

Reminders

What’s due today, tomorrow and later, with anything overdue at the top.

Calendar

Every reminder on its day, by month or by week.

Kanban

Every note with a status, in three columns: to do, in progress and done.

Files

PDFs, audio, video and documents from all your notes, with a built-in PDF reader.

Moodboard

All your photos on one board, each with its own color palette.

What’s new

Version 0.4

Calendar, kanban, a three-color status and a new icon.

  • Calendar: each reminder on its day, by month or week; drag it to change the day.
  • Kanban: to do, in progress and done; drag cards between the columns.
  • Three-color status: red, yellow and green, on any note (notes with a reminder get it automatically), also through Claude.
  • A new icon.
  • Faster sync: notes upload to Drive in parallel, and reordering only sends what changed.
  • Long titles wrap, and search always stays in the same place in the toolbar.

In 0.4.4: an icon without a backplate on Linux and phone tweaks (selection and margins). In 0.4.3: a new icon and a subtler selection dot on cards. In 0.4.2: status on any note. With a reminder it appears on its own; without one, just pick it from the Status menu and the note joins the kanban board. In 0.4.1: the icon without the blue border on macOS.

Earlier versions

0.3: an editor with headings, quotes, code and tables; paste Markdown and screenshots straight into a note; a short ID on every note so you can refer to it when talking to Claude.

0.2: Android (preview); categories sorted by name, by note count or by hand; a sidebar that turns into pills; the window remembers its size; batched sync that carries the card order along.

If you already use Ideario, updates arrive on their own: the app lets you know when it opens. Every version is on GitHub.

Features

Everything in one note.

And every note shows up where you need it: in the list, on the calendar, on the kanban board or on the moodboard.

Markdown notes with photos, checklists and attachments

Text, checklists, side-by-side photos, PDFs, audio and video live together in the same note, each one a block you can drag wherever you want.

  • Checklists with nested sub-items; checking an item checks everything inside it
  • Record audio or take a photo without leaving the note
  • Headings, quotes and tables; paste Markdown and it turns into formatting
  • Paste a screenshot straight into the note
  • Paste a link and it becomes a card with a title and image
  • A big cover emoji to spot the note from afar

Reminders that actually remind you

System notifications right on time, with Snooze and Done. Reminders that repeat every day, week, month or year. If your computer was off, Ideario tells you what you missed as soon as it opens. Every reminder has a three-color status: red (to do), yellow (in progress) and green (done).

Calendar

The same reminders, each on its day, by month or by week. Drag one to another day and the date changes (the time stays); tap a day’s + to create a reminder on it. Repeating reminders already show their next occurrences.

Kanban

To do, in progress and done, in three columns. Drag a card from one column to another or tap its color selector. Claude can do all of this too.

A moodboard of your photos

Every image from every note on a single board, each with its color palette. Filter by tone and find that reference without remembering which note it was in.

When a note grows, it becomes a document

One click and the note opens full-page, with a comfortable reading column. Export it to Markdown, HTML or PDF, with its photos and attachments.

Claude

Works with Claude.

Ideario has a built-in MCP server for Claude. Connect it to Claude Desktop or Claude Code in one click, with no server of ours in between.

Just ask

Connect Ideario to Claude Desktop or Claude Code with one click and ask: “make a shopping list”, “check off the milk”, “what did I write about the trip?”. Claude talks to Ideario right on your computer, with no server of ours in between, and PIN-protected categories stay out of reach. Every note has a short ID (#a1b2c3d4): copy it and say “in note #a1b2c3d4, build a table of the expenses”.

On any screen

Fits any window size

In a large window, a sidebar with categories and tags; close it and categories become pills above your notes. In a narrow window, the layout adapts, with the views in a bar at the bottom. Light, dark or system theme, with the accent colors of your Windows, macOS or Linux desktop.

And more

The details you’ll miss elsewhere.

Many at once

Select notes with the mouse, Ctrl or Shift and change the color or category, or archive them all together, with Undo.

Right-click everything

Cards, reminders, files and categories each have their own menu, on right-click or long-press.

Sync with Google Drive

Through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, across computers and your phone, down to the card order. No server in between, no new account.

Private categories with a PIN

Hide a whole category from lists and search, and require a PIN to open it. Sort by name, by note count or by dragging.

Coming from Google Keep?

Drop in your Google Takeout file and your notes come in with photos, colors, labels and dates.

Local backup

One file with everything, whenever you want or every week. Restoring merges with what’s already there.

Fast search

Accent-insensitive, matches parts of words, across thousands of notes, the instant you type.

Lightweight photos

Photos are resized and stripped of location data by default. Want the original? It’s one setting away.

Download

Free, and it updates itself.

Installers for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android are on the GitHub releases page.

Windows

Download the -setup.exe and install it. When a new version comes out, the app lets you know when it opens.

Download

macOS

Download the .dmg (Apple Silicon and Intel). The first time, right-click the app and choose Open: it isn’t signed by Apple yet.

Download

Android preview

Download the _android.apk on your phone and open it; Android asks you to allow installing apps from that source. Reminders fire even with the app closed; share from another app to create a note. To update, install the new one over it or use Obtainium.

Download

Linux

Install from the repository and Ideario updates along with the rest of your system. Ubuntu, Debian and derivatives:

curl -fsSL https://ideario.pages.dev/linux/ideario.gpg | sudo tee /usr/share/keyrings/ideario.gpg > /dev/null
echo "deb [signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/ideario.gpg] https://ideario.pages.dev/apt stable main" | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/ideario.list
sudo apt update && sudo apt install ideario

Fedora:

sudo curl -fsSL -o /etc/yum.repos.d/ideario.repo https://ideario.pages.dev/linux/ideario.repo
sudo dnf install ideario

Or download the .AppImage, which runs on any distribution and also updates itself.
